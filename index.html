Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers about our location, ordering options, hours, and dishes.

Where exactly is Chito's Restaurant located? +

We are conveniently situated at 35 Plaridel St., Lucban, Philippines, 4328. We are easily accessible from Lucena and the main Lucban town proper, making us an ideal stop for tourists visiting the Lucban Church, Kamay ni Hesus, and local fiestas.

How do I order for curbside pickup? +

Simply call our phone line at (042) 540 9262 or submit an inquiry using our online form below. Let us know your preferred pickup time and vehicle details. When you arrive at Plaridel St., our staff will hand your order to you without you having to find street parking.

What are your business operating hours? +

Chito's is open daily to serve you from breakfast through dinner (typically 8:00 AM to 9:00 PM). During holiday seasons and the Pahiyas Festival, we recommend ordering in advance.

Can we customize pizza toppings or order party bilao sets? +

Yes! Our specialty pizzas (Lodi Hawaiian, Werpa Pepperoni, Rapsa Supreme, Ultimate Sisig, and Veggie) are available in 8-inch, 10-inch, and 12-inch sizes. We also prepare party bilaos of Pancit Habhab and BBQ platters with 24 hours advance notice.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept Cash, GCash, and local bank transfers. For curbside pickup, you can pay via GCash upon confirmation or cash upon delivery to your vehicle.
